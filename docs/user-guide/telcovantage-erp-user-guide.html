1.2The workspace layout

Every screen shares one frame: a sidebar on the left to move between modules, and a header across the top for search, tools, and your account. You only see the menus your role allows.

LEFT

Sidebar

Dashboard, Vendors, CRM, Projects, Assets, Accounting, Invoices, Reports, HR, Documents, Compliance, and (for superadmins) Audit Logs. Settings is pinned at the bottom.

TOP

  • 01Press Ctrl+K (⌘K) to search across vendors, POs, customers, invoices, documents, and more — with quick-create shortcuts.

  • 03Use the header sun/moon toggle for light or dark; admins set richer themes in Settings → Appearance.
Your profileOpen the avatar menu → Profile Settings to change your display name, photo, and password. Your email and role are managed by an administrator.

Chapter 03
Vendors & Accreditation

Accredited and ready.

Onboard suppliers, collect their accreditation documents, and keep their status current. A vendor must be active with an approved Signed NDA before you can issue purchase orders to them.

3.1Adding a vendor

Who can do thisSuperadmin, Admin, Operations
  1. On the vendor list, click Add Vendor.
  2. Under Company Information, fill in the Company Name (the only required field), plus address and TIN.
  3. Add the primary contact, then Banking & Terms (bank details, payment terms, currency).
  4. Click Save Vendor. The vendor is created as Pending and opens their profile.
Pending vs. ActiveNew vendors can't receive POs until an authorized user activates them (header → Activate Vendor) and their Signed NDA is approved.

Upload & approve

  1. In a document row click Upload (or Update to replace). It's saved as Submitted and versioned automatically.
  2. An authorized user clicks Approve, sets an expiry date (required), and confirms — the document becomes Approved.
Who can approve documentsSuperadmin, Admin, Operations · Rollback a version: Admins only

3.3Requesting documents & contracts

  • 01Request Documents emails the vendor a secure upload portal link (everything not yet approved is pre-selected).
  • 02Generate Portal Link copies a secure link you can share manually — vendors upload without dashboard access.
  • 03Vendors → Contracts stores MSAs and formal agreements via New Contract.
Deleting a vendorOnly a Superadmin can permanently delete a vendor, and it can't be undone. In daily work, deactivate instead.

Chapter 04
Purchase Orders

Create once.
Track every step.

Create a PO, run it through approval, issue it to the vendor, and track billing. A PO isn't sent to a vendor until an executive approves it.

The lifecycle

Draft  →  Pending Approval  →  Issued  →  Partially Paid  →  Paid  ·  Overpaid  ·  Cancelled

TelcoVantage ERP · Purchase Orders08

4.1Creating a draft PO

Who can do thisSuperadmin, Admin, Operations
  1. Click Create PO, then pick the Vendor (and optional Project) and set the issued/due dates and terms.
  2. Add Line Items — Item Code, Description, Qty, UoM, Unit Price. The total shows live in the vendor's currency.
  3. Optionally add Sites & Details, then click Create PO. It saves as Draft.
The compliance gateYou can only create a PO for a vendor who is Active with an approved Signed NDA. If either is missing, the form blocks creation. Fix it on the vendor's Accreditation Docs tab, or (with permission) request a waiver — which needs executive approval before the PO can issue.

4.2Approve, issue & document

  • 01Submit for Approval and choose an admin approver (you can't pick yourself).
  • 02An admin clicks Approve & Issue — the PO becomes Issued and is emailed to the vendor automatically.
  • 03Use the Download menu for PDF or DOCX, or Edit DOCX to customize wording and save it back.
Completion certificatesOn the PO page, submit a % Complete certificate; once an admin approves it, that percentage sets the billing ceiling for supplier invoices (see Chapter 5).

Chapter 05
Supplier Invoices & Payments

Match the invoice.
Protect the payment.

Record what vendors bill against their POs, then track and pay it. This is the Accounts Payable side of the business.

  • 01Invoices link to the right PO — pick the vendor first to reveal their open POs.
  • 02Amount guards catch overbilling beyond the PO or approved completion percentage.
  • 03Due dates and payment status stay visible across the whole list.
Who works hereInvoices & payments: Superadmin, Admin, Finance. Payment Requests: Superadmin, Admin, Operations.
TelcoVantage ERP · Invoices & Payments10

5.1Recording & scanning an invoice

  1. Go to Invoices → Record Invoice. Pick the Vendor first to unlock the Related Purchase Order list.
  2. Enter the number, amount, and date (the due date fills from the PO's terms), then the mode of payment and expense category.
  3. Or drag a PDF/photo into scan — OCR fills the fields and matches the vendor & PO. Always review, then Record Invoice.
Overbilling is blockedBilling is capped at the PO amount, or at the approved completion percentage if a certificate exists. The error tells you exactly how much is left to bill.

5.2Payment Requests & recording payment

  • 01A Payment Request (PR) — created on the PO page — authorizes a subcontractor to bill up to a set amount; Finance approves it.
  • 02To pay, open the invoice → Record Payment: amount, date, method, and a reference number (required unless Cash).
  • 03Attach both the Payment Voucher and Proof of Payment (both required); the Official Receipt can be added later.

Chapter 06
CRM · Client POs · Client Invoices

The customer side.

Manage accounts, track the POs clients issue to TVPH, and bill and collect receivables.

Who works hereCustomers & projects: Superadmin, Admin, Operations. Client invoices & payments: Superadmin, Admin, Finance.

6.1Adding a customer

  1. Click Add Customer; enter the Customer Name (required), address, TIN, and status.
  2. Add at least one contact (required); mark one as primary. Click Save Customer.
TelcoVantage ERP · CRM12

6.2Client POs & client invoices

A client PO is a purchase order a customer issues to TVPH; client invoices bill against it and track collections.

  1. Under CRM → Client POs, click Record Client PO — choose the client, PO number, received date, currency, and amount.
  2. Under CRM → Client Invoices, click Record Invoice, pick the client and their PO, and enter the invoice details.
  3. On the invoice, click Mark as Sent, then Record Payment when the client pays (reference required unless Cash).

Client POs — received → partially billed → fully billed.

Client Invoices — draft → sent → paid, with payment history.

Over-billing is blockedYou can't invoice a client for more than their PO amount — the system shows how much has already been billed.

Chapter 07
Projects & Contracts

Tie the work together.

Projects connect the commercial and delivery sides — the customer, the vendors doing the work, the contract file, and the connected purchase orders.

Who can manage projectsSuperadmin, Admin, Operations
  1. Click New Project; enter the name, optionally link a client, set status, and attach a contract document.
  2. On the project page, Link Vendor to attach suppliers; each shows their PO count.
  3. Review Connected POs and Progress & Billing — completion percentage and a per-PO breakdown.
Two kinds of contractThe project's own Contract Document stores one file on the project; the formal legal agreement registered against a vendor lives in Vendors → Contracts.

Chapter 11
AI Assistant & Imports

Ask, and import.

A helper on every screen, plus AI-assisted spreadsheet import for vendors and customers.

11.1The TVPH Assistant

Open the floating helper in the bottom-left corner to reach the TVPH Assistant.

  • 01Ask in plain language — "Who are our top vendors?", "List pending POs", "What's our compliance score?"
  • 02Attach a file with the paperclip to analyze or import it; the assistant knows which page you're on.
  • 03It can create vendors, customers, and draft POs — but always shows a summary and waits for your yes.
Same rules applyAnything the assistant creates follows the same permissions and controls as doing it by hand — a drafted PO still needs an active vendor with an approved NDA.

11.2Importing from a spreadsheet

  1. On the Vendors or CRM list, click Import and drop a .csv or .xlsx file.
  2. Review the Column Mapping — auto-matched, with AI suggestions for anything unclear. Adjust as needed.
  3. When validation passes, click Confirm & Import to see created / updated / error counts.
Required columnsVendors need a Vendor Name; customers need a Company Name. Everything else can be mapped or ignored.

Appendix B
Glossary

Key terms.

AccreditationThe 14-document vetting a vendor completes to become an approved supplier.
AP / ARAccounts Payable (money owed to vendors) / Accounts Receivable (money owed by customers).
Client POA purchase order a customer issues to TVPH (the receivables side).
Completion CertificateA record of the percentage of work done on a PO; the approved percentage sets how much can be billed.
EWTExpanded Withholding Tax withheld on invoices.
NDANon-Disclosure Agreement. An approved Signed NDA is required before a vendor can receive POs.
POPurchase Order issued by TVPH to a vendor.
PR (Payment Request)Authorization for a subcontractor to submit a progress-billing invoice up to a set amount. In this system, PR means Payment Request — not purchase requisition.
UoMUnit of Measure on a PO line (LOT, PCS, SET, HRS, DAYS, MOS, SQM, LM, KG).
WaiverExecutive-approved permission to raise a PO for a vendor who doesn't yet meet the compliance gate.
201 FileAn employee's personnel document file.
Appendix C
Troubleshooting

I can't create a PO for a vendor

The vendor must be Active with an approved Signed NDA. Fix it on the vendor's Accreditation Docs tab, or (with permission) request a waiver — which needs executive approval before issuing.

My PO won't send to the vendor

Issuing is two steps: Submit for Approval, then an admin clicks Approve & Issue. You can't approve your own PO. If the email failed, use Resend to Vendor.

A payment won't save

Both the Payment Voucher and Proof of Payment are required, and a reference number is required unless the method is Cash. Files must be a PDF or image under 10 MB.

A menu or button I need isn't there

Access follows your role — the app hides what you can't use. Check Appendix A and ask an administrator to adjust your role if needed.
